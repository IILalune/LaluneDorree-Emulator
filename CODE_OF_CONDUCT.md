# Community code of conduct

LaLune Dorée Emulator welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be patient
with beginners, discuss ideas respectfully, and help keep the project a comfortable place to learn.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, discussions, reviews, and pull requests.
- Critique code and ideas without attacking the person behind them.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility when your behavior harms someone.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, sharing someone else's
private information, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, Paul Hoang, through an available private contact
channel. If none is available, open an issue titled **Private conduct contact requested**, with
no names, allegations, screenshots, or other sensitive details, to arrange private contact.
For platform abuse, GitHub's own reporting tools are also available.

Reports should describe what happened and include relevant links or evidence privately.
Information will be handled as discreetly as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

## Enforcement and scope

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock conversations, issue a warning,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on the circumstances. Threats
or severe harassment may lead to immediate restrictions. People involved may request a review
through the same private contact channel. This is a volunteer project, so responses may take time.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly.
