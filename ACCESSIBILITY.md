# Accessibility and inclusion

LaLune Dorée Emulator aims to welcome players with different abilities, backgrounds, and ways
of thinking, including neurodivergent and autistic players and children. People have different
needs and preferences; no single setting or description represents everyone.

This project supports awareness, respectful language, and listening to people about their own
experiences. It is an emulator for play and learning, not a therapy or clinical tool.

## Current options and limitations

The app includes configurable gameplay keys and controller buttons, stick dead-zone and trigger
settings, adjustable volume, slow motion, and multiple display filters. These options may help
some players, but they do not establish that the app is accessible to everyone.

The interface has animated stars and clouds, an intro, and visual effects. Games can also contain
flashing images, rapid movement, or loud sounds. The app does not currently provide a dedicated
reduced-motion mode, and screen-reader support and accessibility-standard conformance have not
been verified. Choose filters and volume to suit your needs; pause or stop if something is uncomfortable.

## Share an accessibility need

Use the **Accessibility feedback** issue form to describe a barrier or improvement. Examples include:

- Keyboard or controller navigation that is difficult or confusing.
- Text size, contrast, labels, or instructions that are hard to read or understand.
- Animation, visual effects, or sounds that are distracting or uncomfortable.
- Difficulty understanding save states, settings, or feedback after an action.
- Problems using assistive technology.

You do not need to share a diagnosis, medical history, age, or other personal information.
Describe what happens and what would make the experience easier. A parent, caregiver, educator,
or support person is welcome to report a barrier without identifying a child.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include a child's name, photo, school, location, medical records,
or contact details. Please use an account and reporting process appropriate for the person submitting
the feedback; children do not need to create an account to have their needs represented.

Accessibility suggestions are welcome and considered alongside other project work. This is a
volunteer project, so improvements and timelines cannot be guaranteed. Everyone participating
should follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and respect people's preferred language
and ways of communicating.
